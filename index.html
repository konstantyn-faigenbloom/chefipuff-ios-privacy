Faigenbloom · Chefipuff: Cozy Kitchen

iOS Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Applies to iPhone and iPad.

On-device data and age group

On iPhone and iPad, Chefipuff stores game progress, preferences, an age group and purchase fulfilment records on the device. We ask for an age group to apply advertising restrictions. We do not ask for an exact date of birth or send the selected age group to a Faigenbloom analytics server. Advertising services receive applicable privacy restrictions.

Rewarded advertising and privacy choices

Optional rewarded advertising is delivered through Unity LevelPlay. Google User Messaging Platform handles applicable regional advertising choices. Advertising providers may process information such as network address, device and app information, ad activity, diagnostics and identifiers, subject to the applicable age and privacy settings. This version does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Where available, you can revisit regional ad privacy choices in Settings → Privacy → Ad Settings. See Unity's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases and restoration

Purchases are processed through Apple In-App Purchase. The game verifies Apple transactions to deliver the requested product, retains purchase records locally to avoid duplicate rewards, and uses Apple's entitlement information to restore eligible purchases. Faigenbloom does not receive your payment card details in the game. Apple privacy policy.

App Store analytics

The iOS game does not send its own gameplay analytics to Faigenbloom or use Firebase Analytics. Apple supplies App Store reports, and may share usage and diagnostics according to your Apple settings. Advertising providers' delivery and reporting described above are separate from game analytics. This iOS version does not provide a Faigenbloom cloud save account.

Ad reports

To report an inappropriate ad, open Settings → Privacy → Report an Ad. If you choose to email support, the draft may include the last ad's time, network, creative identifier and auction identifier. We use the details and the information you choose to provide to investigate the report and respond. The game does not submit a report automatically.

Retention and privacy requests

Game data is kept on your device while you use the app. You can remove that local data by deleting the app. Apple and advertising providers handle their data under their own policies. We retain support correspondence as needed to respond and investigate, and as required by law.

For questions or requests about access, deletion, or consent, email konstantyn@faigenbloom.com. Android players can find the Android privacy policy on our website.
