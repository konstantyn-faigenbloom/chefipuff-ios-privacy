1. Versions covered

Versions through 0.1.11 store progress locally and use simulated advertising rewards and coin purchases. They do not show third-party ads or charge real money. The sections below also cover Android 0.1.12, which adds optional rewarded advertisements and Google Play purchases when available. Older versions retain their simulated behavior.

2. Local game data

Progress, levels, virtual coins, equipment, scores, completion times, language and sound preferences are stored on your device. Versions through 0.1.13 do not create a Faigenbloom player account or cloud gameplay profile. Optional Google saving in later supported releases is described below. Purchase transaction identifiers are also stored locally with the coin balance to prevent duplicate rewards. These records are not automatically sent to a Faigenbloom server. Device or operating-system backups may include them under your platform backup settings.

3. Optional rewarded advertising

In supported monetized versions, players may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement to receive the stated gameplay benefit. The integration uses Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. Advertising services may process IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device/app information and identifiers other than the Android Advertising ID, ad interactions, delivery and diagnostic information. Their purposes include contextual ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention and service operation. Network data is transmitted to those providers when advertising services are initialized or used, even if no Faigenbloom gameplay server is present.

The integration is configured with child-directed restrictions for all players, consent to personalized advertising disabled, and sale/sharing opt-out signals. Android Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions are removed from the Android manifest. We do not request precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access for ads. These controls restrict processing; they do not mean that ad services collect no data.

Read Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity's Privacy Policy. Requests concerning data held by an advertising provider may also be submitted through that provider's privacy resources. For Unity IAP data requests, contact our support; where Unity processes data on our behalf, we will forward a verified request to unity-iap-contact@unity3d.com. Records required by law or for transaction integrity may be retained. The advertising SDK is initialized only after you enable optional rewarded ads in the in-game explanation. Without enabling them, it is not initialized by the game.

4. Google Play purchases

Supported monetized Android versions offer consumable virtual coin packs through Google Play Billing, integrated using Unity In-App Purchasing. Google handles checkout, payment authorization and payment credentials. Faigenbloom does not receive your payment-card number or bank credentials. The game receives purchase/transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase status and a signed receipt to validate the purchase and grant coins. The game uses Unity IAP 5.3.1 for the native Google Play store. Unity D2C/Webshop and Authentication are not enabled; no separate gameplay or purchase analytics platform is initialized. Purchase receipts are validated locally. Google processes store transactions under its own policies. Purchase confirmation occurs after the local grant is saved. Pending or declined purchases do not grant coins. License testers can use Google's test payment methods; other purchases may incur real charges displayed by Google Play.

Coin balances remain local. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local coins and transaction records; confirmed consumable purchases are not automatically restored from Google Play as unused entitlements. Device backup availability depends on the platform. For payment history, refunds and purchase authorization, use Google Play and its applicable policies: Google Privacy Policy and Google Play refunds.

5. Children and families

The game has a mixed audience including children younger than 13. Child-directed advertising restrictions apply to every player, including users of unknown age. No chat, public player profiles, behavioral ad profiling, or Faigenbloom account sign-up is provided. Paid checkout is protected by a parent-facing step in the game in addition to applicable Google Play purchase authentication and family approval controls. Parents should supervise purchases and may contact us about their child's privacy. We do not ask children to send personal information to support.

6. Support, sharing and retention

If you email support, we receive the address and contents of your message and use them to respond. Do not include payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information. Support records are retained only as needed for assistance and applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your local progress or upload it to advertising services. Ad and store providers process their service data under the purposes and policies described above and may have their own retention periods. We may disclose information when legally required.

We use the ID to find and delete associated gameplay, purchase and rewarded video observations. Records otherwise expire within 180 days after receipt. This does not delete local progress or Google Play Games data. If the option is unavailable or you no longer have the ID, email us for help.

To request deletion: email konstantyn@faigenbloom.com with the subject “Chefipuff data deletion”. Describe whether your request concerns support correspondence or advertising-provider data. A parent may act for a child. Do not send payment credentials or a full purchase receipt. We will explain any information needed to identify the records, remove support data we control, and coordinate applicable requests with our service providers. Local progress, coin balances, equipment, preferences and the purchase ledger can be removed through Android Settings → Apps → Chefipuff → Storage → Clear data. This resets the local game; review platform backups separately. Provider processing is subject to its retention policies; records required for legal obligations or fraud prevention may be retained. Requests are handled within applicable legal time limits.

You can decline optional ad rewards and avoid optional purchases. Settings provide the privacy-policy link and an option to disable advertising and exit the game. After restarting, the advertising SDK remains uninitialized unless you enable rewarded ads again. Clear app storage to remove local records, and review operating-system backup settings. There is no Faigenbloom player account to delete. Email us to request deletion of support correspondence; legally required records may be retained. Contact the relevant provider for data it controls. Service connections use providers' encrypted transport; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Optional Google Play Games progress saving

In supported Android releases, you can choose Google Play Games to save and restore your kitchen on another Android device. The game uses the Google Play Games player identifier to associate a save with the selected Google account. Google handles sign-in; the game does not request your Google password or email address. Google's SDK may perform platform authentication independently; choosing guest play disables our cloud-progress operations, not the device's existing Google account.

After you connect, levels, stars, completion times, virtual coins, owned equipment, upgrades, seasoning and one-way purchase-deduplication fingerprints are stored using Google Play Games Saved Games. Raw purchase tokens, passwords, emails, language and sound preferences are not included in the cloud snapshot. The service uses Google's game-app storage associated with your account; it does not give this game access to your other Drive documents. No gameplay save is sent to a Faigenbloom server. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Guest play remains available. In 0.1.14 an arithmetic parent step precedes optional linking. Starting with 0.1.15, the in-game sign-in button opens Google's sign-in interface directly, with the cloud-save explanation beside the button. Google's own child-account and parental-consent restrictions still apply. The game does not bypass them or treat a button press as legally verified parental consent. Conflicting saves require a choice. Balances and consumable items are not added together. Valid saves being replaced may be kept in up to twelve local recovery files; uninstalling/clearing app data removes those local files, subject to OS backups.

Settings → Save progress → Disconnect stops this game's synchronisation and keeps local progress. It does not delete the cloud copy or sign out the Google account on the device. To remove the Google-held saved game, use your Google Play Games account's game-data deletion controls; Google governs processing and retention of its account and platform records. Our support can explain the steps but cannot access or delete a private Google save on your behalf. Contact konstantyn@faigenbloom.com for assistance. A future reconnection can restore a retained cloud copy.

После подключения в Google Play Games Saved Games сохраняются уровни, звёзды, время прохождения, монеты, купленные инструменты, улучшения, приправа и односторонние отпечатки журнала покупок.
